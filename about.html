About SwiftParcel

Moving Your World,
One Delivery at a Time.

From a single courier on a bike to a nationwide parcel network — we've built SwiftParcel around one belief: delivery should be fast, transparent, and dependable.

Our Story

Built by couriers, for the people who need them

SwiftParcel started in 2018 with three couriers and a simple promise: pick up in minutes, not hours. We were tired of watching people wait all day for a delivery that should have taken thirty minutes.

Today we operate across 250+ cities with 1,200+ couriers and a parcel network that moves over 2.4 million shipments a year. But the promise hasn't changed — every delivery is tracked, every courier is accountable, and every customer can see exactly where their item is.

"We don't just move packages. We move promises, deadlines, and peace of mind."

— Amelia Chen, Co‑Founder & CEO
