Quick Answers

Before you reach out

Some of the most common questions we get — answered instantly.

How do I track my shipment?

Log into your customer dashboard and open the "Track Shipment" tab. Enter your tracking ID for live GPS updates.

How do I get a quote?

Use the rate calculator on our pricing page — it gives you an instant estimate for courier and parcel services.

Where are my invoices?

All invoices are available in your dashboard under the "Invoices" tab. Download or bulk‑export anytime.

How do I reschedule a pickup?

Open your upcoming booking in the dashboard and click "Reschedule". Free changes up to 2 hours before pickup.

What if my shipment is delayed?

If we miss the promised window, we automatically issue a refund — no form or call required.

How do I open a business account?

Choose the Business or Enterprise plan on our pricing page, or contact our sales team via this form.

Prefer to book directly?

Create your free account and book a courier or parcel pickup in under 60 seconds — no phone call needed.
