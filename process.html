Two Flows, One Standard

Courier and parcel, handled differently

Both follow the same six stages — but the logistics behind each are tuned for their specific needs.

Courier Flow

Same‑day · City‑wide

Courier deliveries are point‑to‑point. One courier picks up from you and delivers directly to the recipient — no sorting, no delay.

  • 1
    Direct pickup Courier arrives at your door in ~12 minutes
  • 2
    Direct transit No hub — straight to the recipient
  • 3
    Delivery in hours Typical completion in 1–4 hours

Parcel Flow

Nationwide · Multi‑hub

Parcel deliveries move through our regional hub network. Each parcel is scanned at every handoff for full chain of custody.

  • 1
    Pickup & sort Scanned at pickup, routed to nearest hub
  • 2
    Hub transit Automated sorting at 18 regional hubs
  • 3
    Final mile Delivered in 1–5 days depending on service
